# Mr. Brij Blog Voice Brief

Apply to all rewrites. Source: existing posts + mr-brij-brand-guide.

## Open
- Blockquote hook stating practical tension
- 2 sentences on stakes (time, cost, trust, release risk)

## Body
- Direct address: YOU, Lets, we
- Named rituals: STORY KICKOFF, devbox/desk check, Bug bash, 80-20 split
- Real metrics only (500GB→20MB, 8h batch→15min)
- Scenario boxes: "Imagine your team…", "On one project…"
- Bullets for checklists; prose for narrative

## Close
- Reader question
- "Happy Testing :)" when natural

## Fix
- Typos, grammar, truncated SEO descriptions
- Keep personality; no buzzword soup or sales tone
